Introducing Businesses in ChatGPT
Manage your business information, get insights about how your business shows up, and turn interest into action in ChatGPT.
You can also publish a Business Agent to help people explore what you offer.
Customers can ask questions, compare products, and find what fits their needs using information you provide from your business—all in a clearly labeled conversation, separate from ChatGPT’s independent answers.
Available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the U.S.
