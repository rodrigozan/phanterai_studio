Set lighting for image generation
Last updated on Dec 10, 2025
Generate images using various lighting styles with Firefly on mobile.
Note
Extended style components, such as Effects, Color, Lighting, and Camera angle, are only available for Adobe's native image models. Partner models don't support these components.
Android
iOS
Open Firefly on mobile.
On the home screen, tap Text to image.
Enter a text prompt in the Prompt field and select any one of the Firefly image models from the Model dropdown menu.
Tap Additional settings and then select Lighting.
Set lighting style to define how light interacts with the subject in the image.
Browse through the options and choose from the following Lighting styles:
Backlighting
Dramatic Light
Golden hour
Harsh light
Long-time exposure
Low lighting
Multiexposure
Studio light
Surreal lighting
Select the Lighting you want, and then tap the close   icon to confirm your selection.
Tap the Generate  icon to see a set of image variations.
