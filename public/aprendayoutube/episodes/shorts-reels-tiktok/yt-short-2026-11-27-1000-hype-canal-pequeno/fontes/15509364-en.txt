Manage your hyped videos
IMPORTANT:
This feature is only
available in certain countries
at this time.
Your viewers can hype your videos to show their excitement for your content and help your channel get discovered by new audiences. Your viewers can also share that they’ve hyped by creating a public post on the Posts tab and Community tab (if enabled) on their channel, giving your video more visibility.
Learn more about
eligibility for hype videos
.
Allow viewers to hype your videos
Note:
The Hype option will only surface if your channel is eligible. There is an option to opt-out by toggling the “hype” button.
Your channel will be auto-enrolled in free hype with the option to disable.
How to opt out of hype
You can disable hype in YouTube Studio within Advanced settings. If you want to opt out of hype:
Login to
YouTube Studio
on your desktop.
Navigate to the
Settings
dashboard.
Select
Channel
to view your channel settings.
Select
Advanced settings
and you’ll find
Hype
.
Deselect the box next to "Let viewers hype my content."
