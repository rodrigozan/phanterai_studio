Hyping videos eligibility, availability & policies
Important:
This feature is only available in certain countries at this time.
Hype is designed for up-and-coming creators in the
YouTube Partner Program
with 500–500,000 subscribers. If you no longer have access to hype, your subscriber count has grown beyond this range or your channel is no longer in the YPP program.
Your viewers can hype your videos to show their excitement for your content and help your channel get discovered by new audiences. Learn how to
manage your hyped videos
.
How does hyping a video work?
1. Upload a video
For the first 7 days after you’ve published a video, viewers will see the option to hype. When a video is hyped, points are immediately applied to the video.
2. Empower your fans
With hype, viewers can rally around your latest upload and let everyone know it’s worth watching. Videos with the most hype points each week appear on a
country-specific leaderboard
that’s ranked by top hyped videos from the viewers in that country.
3. Watch fans help grow your channel
Along with the top hyped videos being featured on the leaderboard, you may also see them highlighted across YouTube with a special “Hyped” badge
for the first 7 days. After fans hype one of your videos, they make a public post to their Posts tab and Community tab (if enabled). Publicly sharing a post about their hype can help your fans pass the spotlight to your channel and help you get discovered by new audiences. Your viewers can hype a limited number of times at no cost for a specific video that has been published in the last 7 days.
Viewers can redeem Jewels to hype videos beyond the 3 per week limit. If a viewer hypes a video with Jewels within its first 7 days, their handle is added to the recognition section of the video's description.
Managing Hype settings
Hype is currently enabled by default for eligible creators. You can opt out of the feature directly from the YouTube Studio mobile app, in addition to the desktop setting. To learn more on how to disable Hype for your channel, see
Manage your hyped videos
.
Eligibility for channels & individual videos
Eligibility for channels
Channels in the
YouTube Partner Program
with 500 to 500,000 subscribers are eligible for this feature.
You live in Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Korea, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Taiwan, Turkey, United Kingdom, and the United States.
Eligibility for individual videos
Content that is not eligible for hype includes, but is not limited to:
Age-restricted content
Misleading, clickbait, or sensational
content
Unlisted videos
Private videos
Made for kids content
Videos with
copyright claims
Videos with YouTube Giving fundraisers
Live streams or Premieres, while live (available on archived long-form videos afterwards)

Additional information
We are gradually rolling out the option to hype a video. As we are developing the functionality, we want the option to hype a video that is appropriate for all audiences. Accordingly, we reserve the right to revise our policies as necessary and to remove the option to hype from channels and remove content that violates our policies from the leaderboard.
