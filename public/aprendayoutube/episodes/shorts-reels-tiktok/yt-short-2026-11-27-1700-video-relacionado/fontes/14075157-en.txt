Add a related video to your YouTube Shorts

You can add a related video after you upload your Short to help direct viewers to your other YouTube content. A related video allows you to showcase your Videos, Shorts, and Live in the Shorts player.

Subscribe to the YouTube Creators channel for the latest news, updates, and tips.
Add a related video
Sign in to YouTube Studio.
From the left Menu, select Content.
Click the Short you’d like to edit.
Select a Related Video from your channel.
Click SAVE.

Note:

Adding a related video is only available with advanced feature access.
The video you select has to be public or unlisted and must follow our Community Guidelines.
How viewers can interact with your related video

Viewers will see a clickable link below your channel handle. A related video is designed to complement your Short and encourage viewers to watch more of your content.
