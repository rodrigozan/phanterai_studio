Sharing links with your audiences

Creators can share links with their audiences on YouTube, but only certain kinds of links are clickable. Use this page to understand which kinds of links are clickable, and find other helpful resources.

Note: To reduce spam and scam attempts, URLs placed in YouTube Shorts comments and Shorts descriptions are non-clickable.

Channel profile links

Use channel profile links to showcase up to 14 links on your channel page. To understand which channel profile links will be shown on your channel profile, and steps to update them, visit this page.

Note: External links that send users to websites featuring content that violates our External links policy are not allowed on YouTube.
