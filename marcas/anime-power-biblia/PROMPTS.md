# Prompts de imagem do Anime Power Bíblia (definidos pelo Rod em 2026-10-30)

Cada prompt de cena = 1. ESTILO FIXO + 2. FICHA(S) dos personagens presentes + 3. DESCRIÇÃO DA CENA. Textos em inglês.

## 1. Estilo fixo (colar no início de todo prompt)
```
2D Japanese shonen anime key frame in the style of Naruto and Dragon Ball Z, cel-shaded, thick bold black outlines, flat color blocks with hard-edged shadow shapes, large expressive anime eyes with sharp highlights, angular manga-style faces, dynamic dramatic camera angle, speed lines and impact-frame energy, simplified flat-color backgrounds with painted anime sky, warm amber #D89B45 and terracotta #A65D3C palette, deep umber #392B25 shadows, first-century Galilee, vertical 9:16 composition. Not realistic, not semi-realistic, not 3D render, not painterly, not photographic. No text, letters, captions, logos, watermarks or modern objects in the image. Safe hand poses, hands inside sleeves, holding an object or resting naturally; never open fanned hands in close-up; no gore, no supernatural energy beams, no glowing liquid.
```

## 2. Ficha do personagem (exemplo: Jesus)
Toda ficha começa com "shonen anime character design," e termina com "cel-shaded with thick black outlines." Copiar inteira em toda cena em que o personagem aparece.
```
Jesus: shonen anime character design, visual age 33, Middle Eastern olive skin #B98260, sharp oval face, deep brown almond anime eyes #3B281F, shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair #302019 in bold stylized strands, full medium-length dark brown beard #302019, ivory linen tunic #EFE2C6, muted crimson mantle #873D35, brown leather sandals #65432D, cel-shaded with thick black outlines.
```

## 3. Descrição da cena (exemplo: cena 4)
```
Medium close-up of Jesus, calm and intense, looking slightly past camera, hands hidden inside the sleeves of his tunic, strong anime rim light in amber, bold black outlines, hard cel-shadow across the face, subtle speed lines in the background, blurred warm lanterns as flat color shapes behind.
```
Descrição de cena sempre pede: contorno preto grosso, sombra cel dura, speed lines sutis, fundo em blocos de cor chapada.

## Referências (--ref)
Fichas e `jesus-ref` gerados no estilo antigo puxam para o semi-realismo. Antes de rodar as cenas, gere de novo a imagem de referência de cada personagem (inclusive Jesus) com este estilo e use só essas como `--ref`.
